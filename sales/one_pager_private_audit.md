# Affiliate Friction Audit - Private Starter

## Problem

Many affiliate sites already monetize destination posts, but commercial hubs and listing pages often make users take an extra step before they see a clear offer or CTA.

That creates affiliate friction:

Hub/listing page -> destination post -> merchant CTA

The audit focuses on finding where that path can be clearer, more measurable, and easier to prioritize.

## What The Audit Does

- Reviews selected commercial hubs and listing pages.
- Checks whether destination posts already contain monetized links or clear commercial intent.
- Identifies cards/listing items where the CTA path may add unnecessary friction.
- Produces a prioritized opportunity matrix and implementation backlog.
- Includes manual review before recommendations are delivered.

## What The Client Receives

- Short executive summary.
- Opportunity matrix.
- Affiliate friction findings.
- Suggested CTA options.
- Prioritized implementation backlog.
- Optional 30-minute walkthrough.
