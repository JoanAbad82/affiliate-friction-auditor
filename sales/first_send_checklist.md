# First Send Checklist

Before sending any Phase 1.6 outreach message:

- [ ] Confirm the site has commercial hubs/listings.
- [ ] Confirm the site publishes affiliate/offers/coupons/reviews or clearly commercial content.
- [ ] Confirm a public contact route exists.
- [ ] Do not send to personal emails unless clearly public and appropriate for this type of contact.
- [ ] Customize the first line with a real observation about the site.
- [ ] Keep the ask to permission for reviewing 2-3 hubs/listings.
- [ ] Position the offer as a private audit with manual review.
- [ ] Do not describe the tool as public, downloadable, or SaaS.
- [ ] Do not claim guaranteed revenue, rankings, or traffic growth.
- [ ] Do not imply partnership with Open Utility Lab.
- [ ] Do not mention the private repo.
- [ ] Log the prospect in `sales/prospect_tracker_initial_10.csv`.
- [ ] Set status and next action before sending.
- [ ] Do not send more than 5 first messages in the first batch.
- [ ] Wait 4-5 business days before follow-up.
- [ ] Stop if they decline, ask not to be contacted, or are clearly not a fit.
- [ ] Record objections or confusing language after each reply.
