## Positive Signals

- They reply with interest.
- They share 2-3 URLs for review.
- They ask about price, scope, deliverables, or timing.
- They ask for a sample format or discovery call.
- They clarify who should review the offer internally.
- They describe current CTA, affiliate, or tracking issues.

## Negative Signals

- They decline.
- They ask not to be contacted.
- They ask for generic SEO, link building, guest posts, or ads instead.
- They expect free software access, a SaaS login, or a downloadable tool.
- They want guaranteed revenue, guaranteed rankings, or guaranteed traffic.
- They cannot share any URLs or confirm authorization.

## Decision After First 10 Prospects

After the first 10 prospects are contacted or intentionally skipped:

- Continue if at least 2 prospects share URLs or request a concrete follow-up.
- Continue if 1 prospect is willing to pay for the Starter audit.
- Rework messaging if prospects misunderstand the offer as SEO, link building, ads, or software access.
- Rework targeting if prospects understand the offer but do not care.
- Pause if there are no replies and no strong signal after two carefully sent batches.

## When To Improve Product Vs. Messaging

Improve messaging when:

- Prospects misunderstand the positioning.
- Prospects ask "is this SEO?" or "is this link building?"
- Prospects do not understand the hub -> destination post -> merchant CTA path.
- Prospects ask what they receive.

Improve the productized audit when:

- Prospects understand the offer but ask for clearer deliverable examples.
- Prospects need implementation support after the audit.
- Manual review takes too long for the validation price.
- The opportunity matrix is useful internally but hard for clients to act on.

Keep the tool private during this phase. The validation target is paid or qualified demand for the private audit, not public software usage.
