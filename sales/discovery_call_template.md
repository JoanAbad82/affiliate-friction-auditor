# Discovery Call Template

Use this for a 20-30 minute qualification call.

## Opening Script

Thanks for taking the time. The audit is intentionally narrow: affiliate friction optimization for commercial hubs and listing pages. It is not generic SEO, link building, or a public software product.

The goal today is to understand whether your pages have the right shape for a private audit: hubs or listings that point to destination posts, where those destination posts may already contain monetized offers or CTAs.

## Qualification Questions

- What type of site are we discussing: affiliate publisher, review site, coupon/deal site, media commerce section, or something else?
- Which 2-3 hubs or listing pages would be most useful to review first?
- Do those pages already receive meaningful traffic?
- Are you authorized to request an audit for this site?
- Who would use the output: founder, editor, SEO lead, affiliate manager, developer, or agency team?

## Technical Questions

- What CMS or stack powers the pages?
- Can cards/listing items be edited independently?
- Can CTA labels, merchant links, or buttons be added without a full redesign?
- Are affiliate links direct, cloaked, redirected, or managed through a plugin?
- Are there pages that should not be crawled or reviewed?

## Affiliate And Commercial Questions

- Which affiliate programs or merchants matter most?
- Are there merchant or editorial restrictions on CTAs, prices, buttons, or disclosures?
- Do destination posts already include merchant CTAs or monetized links?
- Are there known issues with missing tags, wrong marketplaces, or inconsistent link handling?
- Are there hub/listing pages where you suspect users need too many clicks before reaching an offer?

## Measurement Questions

- Do you currently track clicks from hub/listing pages to destination posts?
- Do you track affiliate outbound clicks?
- Can events be segmented by URL, hub, card, CTA, or merchant?
- Do you have approximate sessions for the candidate hubs?
- Would you be able to compare before/after click behavior if changes are implemented?

## Red Flags

- The site owner has not authorized the audit.
- The site has no commercial hubs or listing pages.
- The client wants generic SEO, link building, or content production instead.
- The client expects a revenue guarantee.
- The client expects access to downloadable software or a SaaS dashboard.
- The client cannot share any URLs for review.
- The site has strict no-crawl requirements that prevent meaningful validation.

## Close / Next Step

If there is a fit:

I suggest starting with the Starter audit: up to 3 hubs/listings and up to 30 destination posts. I will run the internal audit, manually review the results, and deliver a short summary, opportunity matrix, suggested CTAs, and prioritized backlog.

Please send the 2-3 URLs, any affiliate/editorial constraints, and any tracking context you are comfortable sharing.

If there is no fit:

This may not be the right audit right now. It is most useful when a site already has commercial hubs/listings and monetized destination posts.
