# Affiliate Friction Audit - Starter

## Target Client

Affiliate publishers, niche site owners, commerce-content teams, and agencies managing commercial hubs or listing pages where users click from a hub to a destination post before reaching a merchant offer.

## Scope

- Up to 3 commercial hubs or listing pages.
- Up to 30 cards or destination posts reviewed.
- Internal tool run plus manual review of the findings.
- Affiliate friction findings focused on hub/listing CTA paths.
- Suggested CTAs where the destination post already shows commercial intent or monetized links.
- Prioritized implementation backlog.
- Short executive summary.
- Optional 30-minute walkthrough.

## Inputs Needed From Client

- 2-3 hub or listing URLs to review.
- Confirmation that the site owner or operator authorizes the audit.
- Any known affiliate programs, merchant restrictions, or editorial rules.
- Optional analytics context: estimated sessions, current click tracking, affiliate click events, or revenue by URL.

## Deliverables

- Opportunity matrix.
- Affiliate friction findings.
- Suggested CTA opportunities.
- Prioritized implementation backlog.
- Short executive summary.
- Optional 30-minute walkthrough call.

## Timeline

Typical turnaround: 3-5 business days after receiving URLs and context.

## Price Range

Early validation price: 250-400 EUR.

This price may change after validation as scope, demand, and delivery effort become clearer.

## What Is Excluded

- No public software access or download.
- No SaaS account.
- No code implementation.
- No SEO audit, link-building campaign, or content rewrite package.
- No guarantee of affiliate revenue, traffic growth, or merchant approval.
- No full-site crawl unless agreed separately.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The reviewed hubs/listings and destination posts are clearly listed.
- Each finding is tied to a specific URL and recommendation.
- The client receives a prioritized backlog that can be reviewed by editorial, SEO, or development teams.
- Any uncertainty is marked for manual review rather than overstated.

## Optional Upsells

- Additional hubs/listings.
- Implementation support for CTA changes.
- Analytics event plan for measuring clicks and conversions.
- Follow-up validation after changes are deployed.
- Monthly monitoring for selected commercial hubs.
